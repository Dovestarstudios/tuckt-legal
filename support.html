Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? Open tuckt → Settings → Change Password.
How do I cancel my subscription? Go to Settings on your iPhone → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → tuckt → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.
How do I delete my account? Open tuckt → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your data.
Is my financial data secure? Yes — see our Privacy Policy for details on how we handle and protect your data.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature? You can either email us directly at hello@tuckt.co.uk, or open tuckt → Settings → Send Feedback.
