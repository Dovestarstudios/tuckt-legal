Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

1. Who we are

tuckt ("we", "us", "our") provides a personal wealth management and retirement planning app for iOS. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights over it, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Data controller: Dovestar Limited, Doves Barn, Swattenden Lane, Cranbrook, Kent, TN17 3PN. Contact: hello@tuckt.co.uk

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, password (encrypted), date of birth, occupation
  • Financial data you enter: investments (individual holdings or simple total-value entries), crypto, cash, property, pensions (including Defined Contribution and Defined Benefit/Final Salary schemes), debts (credit cards, loans, mortgages, BNPL), income, budget categories, dependants' details (name, type, date of birth), sell/buy transaction records for investments and crypto
  • Optional bank connection data: if you choose to connect a bank account (a feature we make available but never require — you can use tuckt fully by entering figures manually), transaction and balance data is retrieved via our regulated open banking partner (see Section 5)
  • Subscription status: whether you're on our free or paid ("tuckt Pro") tier, and trial status
  • Usage data: app interactions, feature usage, crash reports
  • Device data: device type, OS version, IP address (for security/fraud prevention)

We never require you to connect a bank account, brokerage, or pension provider — every feature in tuckt works with manually entered figures. Connecting accounts is an optional convenience.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the app's core functionality (tracking your wealth, generating insights, budget and retirement planning, wealth reports)
  • To power AI-generated analysis and chat responses — when you use these features, the financial data relevant to your question is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate a response (see Section 5). The more information you choose to add to tuckt, the more complete and useful this analysis can be
  • To send you notifications you've opted into (payment reminders, net worth milestones)
  • To process subscription payments (via Apple's App Store and our subscription management provider, RevenueCat)
  • To improve the app (aggregated, anonymised usage analytics)
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service

4. Legal basis for processing (UK GDPR)

  • Contract: processing necessary to provide the service you've signed up for
  • Consent: for optional features like notifications, bank connection, and marketing communications
  • Legitimate interests: fraud prevention, service improvement
  • Legal obligation: for financial/payment records we're required to retain (see Section 6)

5. Third parties we share data with

  • Supabase (database/authentication hosting) — hosted in the EU West (London) region
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — receives the financial data relevant to a given chat message or analysis request, to generate that response. We access Claude via Anthropic's commercial API (not the consumer Claude.ai product), under which API inputs and outputs are automatically deleted after a short retention period and are not used to train Anthropic's models
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — processes purchase and subscription-status data, linked to the Apple App Store
  • TrueLayer (open banking) — only if you choose to connect a bank account; TrueLayer is directly regulated by the FCA as an Account Information Service Provider. We do not see or store your bank login details — these are handled entirely by TrueLayer under open banking standards
  • Market data providers (for live price information, when this feature is active) — we send ticker symbols only, never personal data

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Data retention

We retain different categories of data for different periods, depending on the purpose:

  • General account and financial tracking data (the information you enter into the app — assets, budget, pensions, etc.): retained while your account is active, and for 30 days after you delete your account, to allow you to recover your account if you change your mind. After this period, this data is permanently deleted.
  • Payment and transaction records (subscription payments, invoices, and related financial records): retained for 6 years from the date of the transaction, in line with UK legal and tax record-keeping requirements (Companies Act 2006 and HMRC requirements), even after account deletion. This is a legal obligation on Dovestar Limited as a UK company, separate from your general app data.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to: - Access the personal data we hold about you - Correct inaccurate data - Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten") — noting that payment/transaction records may be retained per Section 6 where we have a legal obligation to do so - Export your data in a portable format (tuckt includes a built-in CSV export feature for this) - Object to or restrict certain processing - Withdraw consent at any time (for consent-based processing, including disconnecting a bank account at any time)

To exercise these rights, contact hello@tuckt.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Data security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to what's necessary to provide the service. Our database provider, Supabase, is SOC 2 Type II compliant, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified, and supports GDPR-compliant deployments.

9. International data transfers

Your data is hosted within the UK/EEA (Supabase, EU West London region). Where we use third-party processors based outside the UK/EEA (e.g. Anthropic, RevenueCat — please confirm their specific data processing locations against their current published policies at the time of publishing), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

10. Children's privacy

tuckt is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Dependant information (including minors) may be entered by an adult user about their own dependants for financial and retirement planning purposes only.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via the app or email.
